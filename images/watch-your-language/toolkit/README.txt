Watch Your Language workshop toolkit

Original printable materials used in the 2023 participatory design workshops
with people with aphasia at Aphasia Re-Connect.

- Watch-Your-Language-Conversation-Cards.pdf
  Twenty context cards used to discuss and compare communication experiences
  across public, social, domestic and healthcare settings.

- Watch-Your-Language-Watch-Sketching-Template.pdf
  Two full-page watch outlines for drawing interface ideas at device scale.

- Watch-Your-Language-Wireframe-Components.pdf
  Three printable sets of common interface components for cutting out,
  rearranging and annotating during low-fidelity prototyping.

Project: https://github.com/HumphreyCurtis/Watch-Your-Language-AAC
Paper: https://doi.org/10.1145/3597638.3608379
